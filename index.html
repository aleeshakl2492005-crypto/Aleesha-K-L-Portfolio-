I work across 3D visualisation, spatial event set design, human-centred experience design, and visual communication.

My Creative Journey

My creative path is driven by transforming imaginative narratives into tangible, photorealistic spaces and memorable brand experiences:

  • 3D Visual Designer Intern at Rainmakers Signature Experiences: Designed 3D stage and set visualisations for grand weddings, festival stages, corporate events, and award ceremonies. Mastered spatial layout engineering, lighting simulations, and structural sightlines under tight production timelines using Blender and Autodesk Fusion.
  • Graphic Design Intern at Inamigos Foundation: Spearheaded digital campaigns, social branding assets, and cohesive visual identities with strong typographic foundations.
  • NIFT Prospectus & Publications: Authored layout designs, art direction, and publication spreads celebrating craftsmanship and design heritage.

Design Interests & Specialisations

• 3D Visualisation & Spatial Environments: 3D Modelling, Event Architecture, Stage Visualisation, Exhibition Stalls, Lighting Rigs, Sightline Engineering, AR/VR Visualisation.
• Experience Design & Visual Merchandising: Brand Activations, Experiential Spatial Flow, Retail Merchandising Systems, Production Design.
• Visual Communication & UI/UX: Human-centred Mobile/Web Design (Figma), Design Systems, WCAG Accessibility, Brand Manuals, Packaging, Editorial Publications.

Design Philosophy

Design is neither purely functional nor purely aesthetic — it is an emotion experienced in physical and digital space. Whether calculating sightlines and load clearances for a 60-foot festival stage, shaping visitor circulation through an exhibition stall, or refining contrast ratios in an accessible health interface, I bridge creative intuition with technical precision.
