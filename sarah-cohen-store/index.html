01 · Who she was

Sarah Cohen

One of the last surviving members of the Paradesi Jewish community in Kochi, Kerala.

Sarah Cohen was known for keeping Jewish traditions alive through her delicate hand embroidery and warm hospitality. Until her passing in 2019, she welcomed visitors to her home and shop, sharing stories of her community’s heritage.

02 · The craft

Indo-Jewish embroidery

A textile tradition that blends Jewish religious motifs with Indian craftsmanship, practised by members of the Paradesi Jewish community in Kerala.

03 · Site study

The present store

Location

Situated in the heart of Jew Town, Mattancherry, Kochi, her shop is a humble corner where history lives. It is part of her home, welcoming tourists, researchers and locals alike.

What she sells

  • Hand-embroidered kerchiefs, tablecloths and Judaica items
  • Traditional Jewish garments and ceremonial cloths
  • Keepsakes reflecting the Paradesi Jewish culture
04 · The brief

Reimagined store

Keep the intimate charm of a shop inside a home, while giving the craft room to be made, seen and bought.

01Increased spaceA slightly larger layout allows better movement and display of products without compromising its intimate charm.
02Dedicated work areasSeparate stitching and embroidery rooms give artisans a peaceful, well-organised place to work, while visitors can witness the craft.
03Organised layoutProducts arranged by function, design and cultural use, making the shopping experience smooth and engaging.
