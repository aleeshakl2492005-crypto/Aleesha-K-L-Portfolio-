logo meaning, p. 14
Graduation is not an end point. It is a release.

The restrained energy in the wordmark expands outward into fluid, layered colour forms: ideas no longer contained in assignments or classrooms, flowing out into the industry and the world.

Tone & Communication Stylep. 6

The voice evolves from playful and exploratory to confident and resolved. Communication moves from raw, hand-drawn expression to bold, structured clarity, the same journey as the students themselves.

Colour Identitypp. 19–22

Five colours, one burst

Bold primaries balanced with warmth, for creativity, diversity and contemporary expression. The scribble letters each take one colour, and the same five paint every ribbon, texture and pattern. Tap any colour to copy its hex.

Secondary tints

For digital interfaces, infographics and illustrations only. Always used tone-on-tone, never as the hero colour.

Accent neutrals

White, black and beige give the primaries room. Books and posters always use white as the main background.

Where can the logo sit?

The manual allows only white, black or beige behind the wordmark. Try a background.

Typography Systempp. 24–26

Two faces, no fuss

A rounded, slightly quirky grotesk for headlines and a calm geometric sans for reading. The expression lives in the scribbles, so the type stays disciplined.

Primary · headlines & wordmark

Coolvetica

  • Optical kerning, tracking 0
  • Leading equal to the font size
  • Sentence case or all caps, never all lowercase
Secondary · paragraphs, captions, information

Euclid Circular A

  • Books: 12 pt on 15 pt leading
  • Kerning 0
  • Used across digital and printed collateral

Visual Languagepp. 28–29

Crayon, scribble, watercolour

The graphic system avoids literal imagery. It is built from three elements, applied with controlled repetition so the playfulness never crowds the content.

Textured colour fields

Flat primaries given a crayon and watercolour grain, so they feel made by hand.

Organic shapes

Fluid, layered ribbons that carry the logo’s energy out to the page edge.

Gestural line work

Loose scribbles and loops drawn over colour, the same hand as the letters.

Incorrect Usagepp. 16–18

Eight ways to break it

The scribbles already look imperfect, which makes the lockup easy to “fix” by mistake. The manual spells out what never to do.

Brand Applicationspp. 31–38

On stage, on screen, on a badge

Every application sits on white or beige, with graphics used sparingly so they keep their impact at any scale. Tap one to open it in the manual.
