01 · About the idea

A building that waxes and wanes

The convention centre is inspired by the moon and its phases, with fluid curves, lighting and ambience reflecting its ever-changing nature.

Designed to transform like crescents and full moons, the space adapts to different event scales and moods. The layout is inclusive and versatile, accommodating conferences, meetings, weddings and social gatherings. An outdoor hall, indoor hall, dedicated dining and exhibition areas are all connected by a central lobby, and each zone can change in form and atmosphere to suit the event.

CrescentIntimate meetings in a single zone
Half moonConferences across the hall and stage
GibbousExhibitions spilling into dining and lobby
Full moonWeddings using every indoor and outdoor space
02 · Layout

One lobby, every event

05 · 3D rendering · Interior

Warm light inside

Lobby & reception

The hinge of the plan

A curved walnut wall wraps the arrival space. The reception desk repeats the crescent: a travertine half-disc under a thin white arc. A backlit sign and a woven bamboo sculpture catch the low sun.

Walnut veneer · Travertine · Backlit acrylic · Bamboo
Dining

A crescent bar

The bar follows the curve of the dining drum, with a cove-lit counter and timber stools. Sculpted planters with round cut-outs echo the moon at the edges of the room.

Oak · Upholstered fabric · Stone floor · Cove LED
Indoor stage

Seats in an arc

Curved tiers of seating face a half-moon walnut stage. A balcony row spans above, reached by two staircases with glass balustrades and lit treads.

Walnut · Travertine · Upholstery · Glass · Step lights
