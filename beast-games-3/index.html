01 · The brief

An arena for
195 nations

Last Flag Standing is a concept for the third season of Beast Games: one contestant from every country, competing in a high-stakes survival arena.

My task was the world they play in. I planned the site, modelled every zone in 3D, and built a lighting and material language that tells the audience what is happening from a single wide shot: pink for energy, blue for strategy, red for elimination.

07 · Designed for play

Two games,
two arenas

Game 01 · Arena 1

Flag Heist

Each contestant hunts for their own country’s flag across a multi-level obstacle arena, but the flags are misplaced. Players can steal, trade and mislead. Inspired by Takeshi’s Castle.

The Divide

Four individual rounds hand out power tokens that quietly build alliances. Groups form in secret and are revealed at the end; the top two survive.
