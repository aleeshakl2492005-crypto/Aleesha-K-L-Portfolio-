01 · Brief & concept

Onam, on wheels

Radio Mango's Thira is an Onam-themed roadshow that brings the energy of Kerala's celebrations to the streets. The promotional truck is a moving visual experience, with tiger-inspired elements, traditional Kerala motifs and bold branding.

Brief
Design a 3D promotional truck for the Thira roadshow, inspired by the energy and cultural vibrancy of Kerala's Onam celebrations, and turn a conventional truck into a visually striking mobile experience that could travel through city roads and draw public attention.

Design concept
The concept reimagines the iconic Spadikam movie truck as an Onam-themed roadshow vehicle, bringing together Malayalam cinema, Kerala tradition and festive energy. Pulikali tiger imagery, vibrant Onam elements and details from traditional Kerala homes, such as sloping roofs and wooden structures, turn the truck into a moving celebration of Kerala’s cinema, culture and Onam.

Fabricated metal and MS framing make up the main structure. Printed vinyl banners and a tiger sticker cover the exterior, LED screens and lighting add movement, and lightweight printed terracotta tiles and fabricated Kathakali-inspired details complete the Onam look.
