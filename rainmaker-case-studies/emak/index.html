01 · Brief & concept

A stage rooted in Thrissur

EMak Silent Hero Awards is an event organised to recognise and celebrate unsung heroes and their contributions. The project involved designing a complete stage setup and creating a 3D visualisation for the awards ceremony held at an indoor venue.

Brief
Create a grand, elegant and visually engaging stage for an award ceremony that complements the scale and ambience of the indoor venue and gives a strong focal point for presentations and award distribution. The work covered the stage concept, the 3D model in Fusion 360, materials, lighting and branding, and the final visualisation.

Design concept
As the awards were held in Thrissur, the stage draws on the cultural identity and visual richness of the city. Elephants, vallam and the vibrant celebrations of Thrissur Pooram were brought into the stage visuals, and traditional motifs and colourful illustrations were combined with geometric patterns and a deep purple palette for a contemporary yet culturally rooted awards-show experience.

Material notes

MDF and fabricated panels form the main structure and layered architectural elements, with CNC-cut panels for the decorative details inspired by Thrissur. Printed graphics and vinyl carry the branding and backdrop, LED screens add moving content, and integrated LED lighting and spotlights add depth and pick out the key features.
