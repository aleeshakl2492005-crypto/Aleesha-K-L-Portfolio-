01 · Brief & concept

A temple for a day

The mandap was designed as a traditional yet elegant ceremonial space inspired by the architecture of Kerala temples and heritage homes, combining warm tones, traditional forms and intricate decoration.

Brief
Design and visualise a 3D wedding mandap stage for an indoor Hindu wedding: a grand yet culturally rooted setting that gives a meaningful backdrop to the ceremony and brings in traditional Kerala architecture and temple-inspired design.

Design concept
Layered roof forms, wooden structures, pillars and ornamental details from Kerala temples and homes shape the stage. Indian mural paintings, traditional diyas and a Tulasi Thara add to its temple character, while floral décor and warm lighting make the setting elegant and sacred.

02 · Layout & measurements

Hall plan

A 100 × 80 ft hall, planned in five 20 ft bays. The mandap fills the centre three bays at the head of the hall; a single carpeted aisle runs from the entrance steps to the stage between the guest seating.

Material notes

MDF and plywood form the structural framework, arches, pillars and decorative panels, finished in wood-textured laminates and paint to reflect Kerala temple and house architecture. Printed mural panels, flowers, artificial foliage, fabric and diyas add richness, while warm LED strips, spotlights and hanging lamps light the details for a soft, ceremonial ambience.
