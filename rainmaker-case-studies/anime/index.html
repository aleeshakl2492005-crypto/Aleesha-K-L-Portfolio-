01 · Brief & concept

A birthday in an anime world

A vibrant, immersive celebration space inspired by the expressive world of anime: bold colours, stylised graphics, character-inspired visuals and dramatic stage lighting, made as a backdrop for photographs, performances and guests.

Brief
Design a unique birthday stage on an anime theme that reflects the energy and imagination of anime while staying a cohesive event setup. It was developed in 3D with themed backdrops, decorative elements, lighting and spatial detailing.

Design concept
Based on the client's preferences, the stage takes elements inspired by Naruto and Pokémon, with vibrant colours, bold graphics, layered backdrops, themed props and dramatic lighting. To personalise it, the child's face was digitally generated and placed into selected character visuals, turning the stage into a fun animated world.

Built in layers

The stage reads front to back as a set of layers: plinth and steps, character cut-outs, the gates and arch, then the blossom canopy and truss overhead.
