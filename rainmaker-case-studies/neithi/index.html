01 · Brief & concept

Products you can play with

Neithi is a hair and skincare brand centred around natural beauty and self-care. The stall reflects the brand through organic forms, natural elements and engaging product displays.

Brief
Conceptualise and develop a 3D exhibition stall that represents the brand and displays its hair and skincare products. The project covered space planning, product display design, material and lighting selection, branding, 3D modelling and animation.

Design concept
The stall is built around interactive product discovery. A Spin the Bottle activity, bottle-shaped product shelves and an oversized spinnable under-eye gel model make the products part of the space itself. A mirror and trial area lets visitors try the samples they receive, and digital displays carry the brand story.

Material notes

MDF and plywood form the main structure, shelves and counters, finished in white paint and laminate. Acrylic and neon signage carry the branding, while cane furniture, artificial plants, printed graphics and the patterned carpet add warmth to the soft, organic concept. Warm LED strips and pendant lights set the ambience.
